How it works & costs

The browser decodes the video with a plain <video> element and samples frames onto a canvas, keeping only frames where the list actually moved — typically 15–30 frames from a 50-second recording, about 400 KB total instead of a 250 MB upload. Those frames go to a vision model that reads rank, name and points, and also writes each stylised name out in plain letters. That plain form is fuzzy-matched against your roster, so 𝓟𝓸𝓶-style tags resolve without anyone retyping them. Roughly a fifth of a cent per recording on Gemini Flash, and inside the free tier in normal use.
